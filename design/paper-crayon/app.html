Paper Crayon

A sketchbook come to life. Warm paper, wobbly ink outlines, crayon fills and washi tape holding the cards to the page — charming, tactile, and never childish. Handwriting carries the joy; Nunito carries every number, label and paragraph so the data stays honest.

Open the system reference

Eight screens, one engine. Everything outside the board area — status bar, HUD, nav, cards, buttons — is shared, so a new game only has to supply its board. Paper Crayon is the direction that makes an offline, account-free app feel like a gift rather than a utility.
